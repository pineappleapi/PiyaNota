## 2. Ground rules

- `piyanota_dataset` is TEST only: no training, model selection or threshold tuning. The reading is one-time per checkpoint; the PREREG is frozen and hashed before any checkpoint scores on it.
- GT built from an agent, or edited from a PDF check, is diagnostic per CLAUDE.md unless the user accepts it as scoring GT at the freeze. Ask at step 7.
- Do not open PDFs that carry a "do not open" rule for the author without the user's lift (A7 14.3 notes this for Pangarap and Gaano); ask first.
- Bash tool refuses compound commands; use single commands and the Edit/Write tools.

## 4. Risks

- Agent-made or PDF-edited GT may encode its own errors; the choral-conductor check is the control, and the result stays diagnostic unless the user accepts it.
- Only 10 pieces, chosen for divisi, so all results are descriptive.
- Per-bucket floors (layer bars 600; 2+ ledger and template heads 300) may not be met; those buckets are "underpowered, report only".
- Memory: run one scoring process at a time (the 2026-11-06 parallel runs were killed for low memory).
- The real-photo CRNN domain gap (BUG-011) is open; photos of prints add camera effects the renders never had.
