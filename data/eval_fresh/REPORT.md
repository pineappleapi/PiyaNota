# Fresh-photo evaluation: Stage 3 with and without the clef/key classifier (2026-09-28)

Pre-registration: `PREREG.md` (written before any Stage 3 output). All numbers: `REPORT_tables.md`
(generated by `make_tables.py` from `runs/scores/*/*/score.json`). Confusion matrices: `runs/scores/<piece>/<crnn>/cm_*.png`.

## What was run

- **Pipeline:** the current training-tree `stage1_preprocessing.py`, `stage2_detection.py` and
  `stage3_transcription.py` (HEAD `78c35d8`, unchanged; hashes in `runs/run_meta.json`), on the real phone photos
  of four pieces. None were used to train or select the CRNNs or ClefKeyNet. **Exception, Stage 1 only:**
  Honneur and Handog are in the Stage 1 non-staff-ink mask TUNING split (`training/step3_mask_split.json`), so
  their Stage 1/2 output is not a clean held-out measurement. Every downstream number on those two pieces
  (SER, pitch, precision) is computed on strips from the tuned mask and inherits this. Non Nobis and Gaano are
  not in the tuning split.
  Everything ran on CPU and is deterministic. For every strip the (a) events reproduce `process_strip` exactly
  (0 failures across 12 runs).
- **Amendment 1 (PREREG.md):** Gaano Ko Ikaw Kamahal and Handog were added on 2026-09-28, with their GT hashed
  before any pipeline run on them. The only code changes were the piece lists, labels and the note precision/F1
  columns. Re-scoring left every Non Nobis and Honneur number unchanged (checked against the saved summary).
- **Stage 3, two versions, same CRNN tokens:**
  - **(a) as-is:** the CRNN reads the clef and the key.
  - **(b) + classifier** (`stage3_clefkey.py`): ClefKeyNet reads the clef and the key.
    - `resolve_clef` is the only thing that sets the octave.
    - Each system's key comes from a confident-majority vote.
    - Low-confidence staves are flagged `review_needed`.
- **CRNNs:** the two font-fine-tuned seeds (reported as a range, never one seed alone) and the original
  Camera-PrIMuS CRNN as a reference row.
- **Oracle row:** the ground-truth clef and key sent through the (b) path. This is the ceiling for any
  clef/key reader with these CRNN tokens.

- **Gain (b) - (a), pitch accuracy:** +53.8 to +56.0 points; the paired 95% CIs are [39.3, 68.5] and [40.9, 71.3].
- **Pre-registered gate: PASS for both seeds.** All 4 criteria pass: silent-wrong (b) ≤ (a), 0 double-octave
  staves, gain ≥ 20 points, review ≤ 25%.
- **(b) equals the oracle** on every metric. With these CRNN tokens, nothing is left to gain from better clef/key
  reading on this piece. The remaining error is the CRNN's note and rhythm reading.
- **This piece does not test the classifier itself.** Every Non Nobis staff uses its voice's standard clef
  (S/A treble, T treble-8vb, B bass), and the key is 1♭ throughout. A voice-default clef rule plus the correct
  key would score the same 100% and equal the oracle, and `resolve_clef` falls back to that default anyway.
  The 28/28 clef and key result amounts to about 7 system-level key decisions on one key (Wilson 95% lower
  bound on 28/28 is about 88%). ClefKeyNet also failed its own real-photo gate (BUG-021). What Non Nobis does
  show is the effect of replacing the CRNN's clef/key reading, which is what version (a) depends on.
- **Only the header changes.** (a) and (b) use identical note, rest and barline tokens. All of the pitch gain
  and the SER drop come from replacing the clef and key header; rhythm accuracy is unchanged.
- **Pitch accuracy is recall-like.** The alignment never penalises extra predicted notes, and the CRNN emits
  354 notes against 275 GT notes on this piece (1.3× overall; about 1.7× on the detected strips). Note
  precision (table above) is the check. This inflates the absolute numbers for (a) and (b) alike.
- **Baseline CRNN (reference):** pitch accuracy 6.9% → 52.4% end-to-end (70.2% detected) and SER 72.4% → 62.3%.
  The fine-tuned CRNNs beat it with (b) (64.0% vs 52.4%), mainly through rhythm (63.5-64.8% vs 47.8%).

## Deviations from PREREG.md

1. **Added reporting (not a gate change):** per-staff counts of silently wrong **clef** and silently wrong
   **key**. They were added after the first scoring. The pre-registered "silent-wrong clef/octave" metric only
   counts median offsets that are multiples of 12. It showed 0 for (a), even though (a)'s clefs are almost all
   wrong: a C-clef misread shifts pitch by a non-octave interval. The gate still uses the pre-registered metric
   and its result does not change.
2. `tie_unresolved` (diagnostic 7) was not computed, because Stage 4 is not part of this run.
3. Note precision and F1 were added after ml-cv-lead's review (reporting only, computed from the stored
   per-strip counts). The GT-clef/key facts above were also checked then.
4. For (a), the review_needed rate is 0 by construction: Stage 3 as-is has no flagging. It is shown as "not
   flagged", not as a success.

## Known issues found or confirmed

- **BUG-024 (open):** Stage 3 stacks an explicit ♯/♭ on top of the key alteration. It affects (a), (b) and the
  oracle equally, so it lowers the ceiling but does not change the comparison.
- **Known limit (pre-registered):** in (b), a real accidental on the first note right after the key signature
  is dropped along with the CRNN header. The self-test on GT tokens showed 2 such pitch misses on Non Nobis,
  shared with (a).
- **BUG-025 (open):** the ClefKeyNet crop x0 lands wrong under page-edge shadow (Honneur p3). These photos are
  TEST-only, so any fix must be developed on other pages.
- **Stage 2:** it cannot handle a system with an extra solo staff (Non Nobis p4) or divisi systems of 5-7 staves
  (Handog, 9 of 14 pages; BUG-026). When one such system shares a page with a normal 4-stave system, the whole page is
  lost. That is outside the strict 4-stave SATB layout the pipeline assumes.
- **Note over-production (all pieces):** on the detected strips the CRNN emits about 1.7 times as many notes as the GT (354 vs 205; Honneur: 0.94-0.96; Gaano 1.12-1.14; Handog 1.89-1.96).
  This is partly explained by GT chords being scored as single OOV wildcards, and is not investigated further.

## Limits of these results

- The test is 4 pieces and 188 scored GT strips (36 + 28 + 72 + 52) from one photographer. Non Nobis alone decides the gate, and its
  95% CIs span about 30 points. Results hold "on these photos", not in general.
- The Honneur, Gaano and Handog GTs are agent-made and not user-verified; Gaano and Handog were not checked at
  all before the run. Their numbers are diagnostic and are never pooled with Non Nobis.
- Honneur and Handog are in the Stage 1 mask tuning split, so none of their results are fully held out.
- Timing: `stage3_clefkey.py` was last saved at 02:35 and PREREG.md at 02:36 (2026-09-28). The first Stage 3
  output was written at 02:40, so both predate every result. The version (b) hash is in `runs/run_meta.json`.
- "Verified by the author" means the developer checked the transcription, not an independent annotator.
  Chords and divisi in the GT are OOV wildcards and are not scored as pitches.
- All four pieces use only standard voice clefs, so clef reading beyond the voice default is untested. One
  mid-piece key change (Gaano, 3 flats to 1 flat) was read correctly; that is a single case.
- The review rate on Non Nobis (3.6-7.1%) is optimistic, because its clefs and key are canonical. On Honneur it
  was 39%.
- Absolute quality is still low. On Non Nobis: SER about 50%, rhythm 64% end-to-end, half rests 4/13.
- The fine-tuned CRNNs and ClefKeyNet were not trained, selected or tuned on these photos. The only numbers
  chosen after seeing the data are the extra silent-wrong counts (deviation 1), which are reporting only.
- Pitch accuracy is measured after edit alignment, so a note that is inserted or deleted but otherwise
  correct is not penalised twice. SER covers insertions and deletions.

## Suggested abstract paragraph (revised after ml-cv-lead review: OK-with-changes)

> On phone photos of one unseen open-score SATB piece (Non Nobis Domine; 36 staves, 275 notes; ground truth
> transcribed and checked by the author, with chords excluded from pitch scoring), replacing the CRNN-CTC
> transcriber's own clef and key reading with a dedicated clef/key classifier plus voice rules raised end-to-end
> pitch accuracy from 8-10% to 64% (95% CI about 48-79%; two fine-tuning seeds). On the 28 staves the staff
> detector found, it reached 86%; the detector failed on one page with an extra solo staff. The note and rhythm
> tokens were unchanged, so all of the gain comes from correcting the clef and key header. Rhythm accuracy stayed
> at 64% and symbol error rate only fell from 58% to 50-51%, which leaves note reading as the main source of
> error. Pitch accuracy does not penalise extra notes (note precision was 50%). This piece uses only standard
> voice clefs and one key, so a voice-default rule would score the same, and these results do not test the
> classifier's accuracy on its own. On a second piece with unverified ground truth, the gain was similar (11-15%
> to 73-76%), 2.6-3.4 points below the ground-truth-clef ceiling. However, 39% of its staves were flagged for
> review, mostly because a crop-placement fault on one shadowed page cut into the clefs. These are results on 2
> pieces from one photographer, not general performance.
